// About me

Mechanical engineer, maker & problem-solver.

I'm a mechanical engineering graduate from Grenoble INP – Génie Industriel, specializing in additive manufacturing, DFM and mechatronic prototyping. I like taking an idea all the way from concept to a functional prototype on the bench.

Along the way I've cut assembly times, improved system reliability and shipped working hardware — during internships at Airbus (leading an assembly-station integration that cut cycle time from 8 to 5 hours) and at FAIRME (redesigning a system for DFM, dropping thermal failure rates from 95% to 5%). I also spent a semester at POSTECH in South Korea, ran my school's FabLab, and took six months for a working holiday in Australia.

Outside the workshop you'll find me doing photo & video, golf, Thai boxing or running. I'm currently seeking a full-time role for Q3 2026 in product development, industrial engineering or technical project management.

2026Personal projectIn progress

Smart plant pot

I love having plants at home, but I'm not always great at remembering to water them — so I designed a connected pot to monitor my plants' health. A capacitive soil moisture sensor integrates directly into a 3D-printed ribbed enclosure, keeping the electronics discreet. For now it's hardware-only: the sensor reads soil moisture and the wiring is ready for a microcontroller. Next step: log the data and send watering alerts.
